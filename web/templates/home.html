Welcome to SOC-Playground

A safe sandbox for security analysts to experiment with local AI models on real SOC work — threat hunting, malware analysis and alert triage — without sending anything to an outside AI service.

What is this?

SOC-Playground lets you download open-source AI models and run them right here, inside your own environment. You can chat with a model, let it use real (read-only) security tools on your behalf, and measure how well different models actually perform — so you can decide which ones are worth trusting for real work.

Everything runs on local models: your prompts and data stay in your environment, not with a third-party AI provider. It's a place to explore and compare — not a production triage system.

Who is it for?

SOC analysts and the security team who want to see, hands-on, how local large language models can help with day-to-day security operations — and who want evidence, not hype, about which model is good enough before relying on it.
